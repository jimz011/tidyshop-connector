# Lists

## Creating a list

Tap **New list** on the home screen. A list starts in one of two ways:

=== "Store"

    Choose a **Country**, then search for the shop. TidyShop names the list after the shop and uses
    its logo as the list's icon. You can still change the name.

    Shops are currently included for the **Netherlands**, **Germany**, **Belgium** and
    **France**, from supermarkets to DIY and electronics stores. Is your shop missing?
    [Ask for it](https://github.com/jimz011/tidyshop-connector/issues).

=== "Manual"

    Give the list any name, such as *Party*, *Pharmacy* or *Weekly groceries*, and choose an icon.
    The icon picker has two tabs: **General** icons and **Stores** logos.

Then pick the **List type**:

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Personal** | Only on this phone. You can still [send someone a copy](sharing.md#send-a-copy). |
| **Family** | Synced through your family server and shareable with the household. Shows **Set up first** until this phone has [joined a family](family.md). |

## The home screen

Your lists are grouped into **Personal lists** and **Family lists**, and within each group by the
country of their shop, with anything else under **Other lists**. Tap a group heading to fold it
away. You can hide a whole group from Home under
[Settings → Home screen](appearance.md#home-screen).

Each list card shows how much is left to buy.

- **Search**: **Find a list** filters your lists by name.
- **Swipe a list card left** to reveal quick actions: **All done** (tick off everything) or
  **Unmark**, **Edit**, and **Delete**.

## Sorting lists

Use **Sort by** on the home screen:

| Option | Order |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | A–Z. Tap it again for Z–A. |
| **Updated** | Most recently changed first. Tap it again for oldest first. |
| **Custom** | Your own order. Press and hold a list, then drag it up or down. |

## Inside a list

- **Swipe left or right** anywhere on the list to jump to the next or previous list, without going
  back to Home.
- Items still to buy are under **To buy**. Ticked items move to **Completed**, where **Clear**
  removes them.
- Lists you own show a **share** button. See [Sharing](sharing.md).

### The list menu

The menu at the top of a list holds:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename list** | Change the name. |
| **Edit list icon** | Choose another icon or store logo. |
| **Quick add settings** | How many suggestion rows to show, and more. See [Quick add](items.md#quick-add). |
| **Mute notifications** / **Unmute notifications** | Stop or restart notifications for this list only. See [Notifications](notifications.md). |
| **Mark all as done** / **Unmark all items** | Tick off, or un-tick, every item. |
| **Clear checked items** | Remove everything under Completed. |
| **Clear all suggestions** | Forget what TidyShop has learned for this list. This can't be undone. |
| **Delete list** | Remove the list and its items from this phone. |

!!! note "Deleting a family list"
    Deleting a family list you own removes it from the family server and from every phone it's
    shared with. Only the owner can do that. If a list someone else owns should disappear from
    your phone, ask them to stop sharing it with you.
