# Appearance

Everything below is under **menu → Settings**.

## Theme and colour

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | System, Light or Dark |
| **Color** (accent palette) | Forest, Mint, Ocean, Indigo, Plum, Rose, Sunset, Amber, Slate or Graphite |

## Typography

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Font style** | Modern or Classic |
| **Font size** | Compact, Standard or Large |

## Home screen

Choose whether **Personal lists** and **Family lists** appear on Home. A hidden group also stays out
of Home search, the totals, and swiping between lists. The lists themselves aren't deleted; show the
group again to bring them back.

## Language

TidyShop follows your phone's language where it can. The interface is English for now; more
languages will follow once it settles.
