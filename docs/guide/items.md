# Items and suggestions

## Adding items

Type in **Add an item…** at the top of a list, then press enter or the plus button.

TidyShop reads a quantity and a size out of what you type, so you don't have to fill in separate
fields:

| You type | Item | Quantity | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bread` | bread | | |
| `5 bread` | bread | 5 | |
| `2x 500gr salt` | salt | 2 | 500 g |
| `1 bread 500gr` | bread | 1 | 500 g |
| `1,5 liters milk` | milk | | 1.5 L |
| `cola 33 cl` | cola | | 33 cl |

The units it understands are **ml, cl, l, g, kg, oz, lb** and **pieces**. Both a decimal comma
and a decimal point work.

## Suggestions while you type

Start typing and TidyShop lists the products it knows that match, for example **br** offers
**Bread** and **Brie**. Tap one to add it straight away, with the department and photo it was
saved with. A product is known once it has been on the list before, or once you gave it a
department or a photo.

If you type a full name that TidyShop already knows and press enter, the department and photo are
filled in for you as well.

## Department and photo

Under the add field, **Details** opens two optional extras:

- **Department**, where the product lives in the shop: Bakery, Dairy, Frozen. Departments you
  have used before appear as buttons.
- **Photo**, taken with your camera (**Take photo**) or picked from your gallery (**Choose**).
  Photos are shrunk to a small size first. **Remove photo** takes it off again.

You can add or change them later too: press and hold an item, or use **Edit** in its **•••** menu.
An item with a photo shows it as a small picture, and tapping the picture enlarges it. The
department is shown under the item's name.

Every product that has a department or a photo is remembered, so the next time you add it the
details come with it. On a [family list](sharing.md#share-a-family-list) the remembered products live on
the family server, shared by the whole household, and the photos are stored there too. Changing
a product's department or photo on one phone changes it for everyone; clearing both forgets the
product. On a personal list, they stay on your phone.

## Moving items to another list

To move one item, open its **•••** menu and choose **Move to…**.

When a shop did not have several things, open the list's **•••** menu and choose **Move open items
to…**, then the list to move them to. Everything not yet ticked off moves, keeping its photo,
department, quantity and size; ticked items stay where they are. You can only pick lists you can
edit.

## Searching a list

The **⌕** button at the top of a list opens a search field. It filters the list as you type,
matching an item's name or its department. Tap **✕** to close it and see everything again.

## Ticking things off

- **Tap** an item to tick it off. It's struck through and moves to **Completed**. Tap it again to
  put it back.
- **Press and hold** an item to edit it.
- The **•••** button on an item offers **Edit**, **Copy to…** (put the same item on another list
  you can edit) and **Delete**.

In the edit dialog, leave the quantity and volume empty to keep an item as a plain entry.

## Quick add

Under the input field, TidyShop shows **suggestions**: the things you add to this list most often.
Tap one to add it. The row scrolls sideways, and **More ›** jumps to the next page of suggestions.

Suggestions are learned **per list**, so your pharmacy list doesn't suggest bananas. On a
[family list](sharing.md#share-a-family-list), everyone with Edit access teaches the same
suggestions, so the whole household sees the same ones.

To remove a suggestion, **press and hold** any suggestion. The row switches to **Remove
suggestions**; tap **×** on the ones you don't want, then **Done**. **Clear all suggestions** in the list menu forgets everything this list has
learned.

### Quick add settings

In the list menu, **Quick add settings** controls:

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Visible rows** | How many rows of suggestions the quick-add area shows (1–7). |
| **Maximum suggestions** | How many suggestions the list keeps in total. |
| **Remember quantity, size and unit** | On: "2 × 1 L milk" is learned as its own suggestion. Off: suggestions are learned by name only. |

## Sorting items

**Sort items** inside a list offers:

| Option | Order |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | A–Z, or Z–A when you tap it again |
| **Added** | First added at the top and last added at the bottom, or the other way round when you tap it again. This is the order new installs start with. |
| **Custom** | Your own order. Use the arrows on an item to move it. |
