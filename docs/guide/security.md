# Profile and app lock

Everything below is under **menu → Profile**.

## Your profile

TidyShop has no account. Your profile is just how you appear, on this phone and to your family:

- **First name** and **Last name (optional)**
- **Email** (optional). It's only used to look up a Gravatar picture, and isn't sent anywhere
  else.
- **Profile picture**: **Choose photo** from your gallery, or **Use Gravatar**. For Gravatar,
  TidyShop sends a one-way SHA-256 hash of your email, never the address itself.

Tap **Save profile** when you're done.

## App lock

Protects your lists from anyone else holding your unlocked phone.

1. **Set up app-lock PIN**: choose a 4–6 digit PIN.
2. **Enable biometric unlock**, if you like, to use your fingerprint or face instead. Set up
   fingerprint or face unlock in Android's settings first. Your PIN always works as a fallback.
3. **Lock after leaving TidyShop**: how long the app may stay unlocked after you switch away:

| Option | Locks |
| --- | --- |
| **Always unlocked** | Never. The default. |
| **Immediately** | As soon as you leave the app. |
| **1 minute** · **5 minutes** · **10 minutes** · **30 minutes** | After that long away. |

**Remove app-lock PIN** and **Disable biometric unlock** undo them.

!!! note "Forgot your PIN?"
    There's no way to reset it from inside the app, which is the point of a lock. Reinstalling
    TidyShop clears it, along with your lists, so keep a [backup](backup.md).

!!! info "Had a password from an early version?"
    Very early versions of TidyShop used a password. If you set one, it still unlocks the app,
    alongside your PIN and fingerprint, and **Change password** appears here. New installs don't
    have a password at all.

## Logging out

**Account on this phone → Log out** is for a phone that should no longer have TidyShop on it. It
deletes **every list on this phone**, your profile, your PIN and your family enrolment, after you
confirm. It can't be undone, so save a [backup](backup.md) first if you want to keep your lists.

Only want to stop sharing with the family? Use **Leave family** instead. See
[Leaving](family.md#leaving).
