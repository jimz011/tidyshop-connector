# Sharing

TidyShop has two ways to share a list. Which one you want depends on whether the other person
should see **later changes**.

| | Send a copy | Share a family list |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Needs a server | No | Yes, a [family server](../server/index.md) |
| The other person gets | A snapshot of the list as it is now | The same list, kept in sync both ways |
| Works with | Anyone with TidyShop | Members of your family |

Both start from the **share** button at the top of a list you own.

## Send a copy

**Share → Send copy to** opens Android's share sheet with a link. Send it through any messenger or
email. When the other person taps it, TidyShop asks **Add shared list?** and, if they choose
**Add list**, creates a personal copy on their phone.

The whole list travels inside the link itself: it isn't uploaded anywhere, and the link works
without a server. That also limits its size. A very long list shows **List is too long to send**;
split it into two lists, or share it through your family server instead.

A copy is a one-off. Changes either of you make afterwards stay on your own phone.

## Share a family list

A **Family** list lives on your family server. [Join a family](family.md) first, then create a list
with **List type: Family**, or open an existing family list.

**Share → Share with family** sets who can see it, in one of two ways:

- **Everyone**: one level for the whole household. It also lets any member add the list themselves
  from **Family sharing → Lists you can join**.
- **Individually**: a level per family member.

The levels:

| Level | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **View** | See the list. Read-only. |
| **Check** | Tick items off and back on, and nothing else. Handy for whoever does the shopping. |
| **Edit** | Add, change and remove items too. |
| **Hidden** | Not shared with this person. |

Family administrators always get **Edit**, whatever is set here. Only the list's owner can
change its sharing or delete it.

Changes sync as you make them. While TidyShop is open, other phones receive them within a second.

## Notify family

Family lists have a **Notify family** bell. Tap it when the list needs attention, for example
"someone please do the shopping". Everyone the list is shared with gets a notification, if they
have notifications turned on. The bell turns into **Family notified** once it has been sent.

To avoid a flood, a list can raise one nudge a minute; tapping again within that minute doesn't
notify anyone a second time.
